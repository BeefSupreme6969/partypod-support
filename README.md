# PartyPod

PartyPod is an Android music player for music on your own [copyparty](https://github.com/9001/copyparty)
server, with classic Artists / Albums / Songs menus, search, an album artwork finder and Android Auto.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- Support and feedback: beefsupreme69696969@gmail.com

## Getting started
1. Open PartyPod and enter your copyparty address (for example `http://192.168.1.10:3923`) and password.
2. Tap **Browse** and pick your music folder, then **Save and scan my music**.
3. For the best results, run copyparty with tag indexing (`-e2ts`) so PartyPod can read artist and album tags.

## Android Auto
PartyPod appears in Android Auto automatically once installed from Google Play.

PartyPod is an independent app and is not affiliated with the copyparty project.
