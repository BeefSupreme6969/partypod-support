# PartyPod Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 17, 2026
**Developer:** Beefsupreme69696969
**Contact:** beefsupreme69696969@gmail.com

PartyPod is a music player for music stored on your own copyparty server. It has no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data.

## What PartyPod stores on your phone
- The server address, username and password you enter, so it can connect to your server.
- A list of the songs on your server (titles, artists, albums and similar tags) so you can browse quickly.
- Recently played songs and album art, cached to make playback smoother.
- Your app settings, such as light or dark mode.

This information stays in PartyPod's private storage on your phone. It is not included in Android
backups and is deleted when you clear PartyPod's data or uninstall the app.

## What PartyPod sends over the network
- **To your copyparty server:** your login, requests to list folders and play songs, and, if you turn it
  on, album artwork that PartyPod saves into your album folders. This server is run by you (or whoever
  gave you access), not by the developer.
- **To public artwork services, only when you use "Find artwork":** the artist and album name of albums
  that are missing artwork are sent to Deezer, Apple's iTunes Search and MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive
  to look up a cover picture. Nothing else is sent, and no account is used. Those services have their own
  privacy policies.
- **Android Auto:** when you connect to a car, song names and artwork are shown on the car's screen
  through Android Auto.

## Security
Your password is stored in PartyPod's private app storage. Connections to your server use whatever your
server supports; use an https address or a VPN such as Tailscale when connecting from outside your home.

## Children
PartyPod is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Clear PartyPod's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Artwork PartyPod saved to your own
server can be deleted there like any other file.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
